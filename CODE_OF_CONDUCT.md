# Code of conduct

Ferrule is a project for people who build software. We want everyone who
takes part to be able to do their best work without being harassed or made
to feel unwelcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people whose first language is not
  English.
- Credit the work of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind, including based on
  age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
  experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers at
**dalersodikov101@gmail.com**. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or
temporarily or permanently ban anyone who violates this code, in proportion to
the harm caused.
